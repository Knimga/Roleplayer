## Southlands

**Borders:** N: [[Midlands]] | S: southern coast | E: [[Tieryf Vale]] | W: western coast

- Laria's oldest claimed ground. Verdant farmland, rich soil — fed the earliest kingdoms.
- Site of [[Kirinar]]'s rise from humble trading town to largest city on the continent.
- The [[Arcane Laws]] originated here before spreading outward.
- Early history was volatile — dozens of small kingdoms rose and fell before Kirinar endured.
- Synonymous, to the rest of Laria, with order, law, and southern influence — reputation is contested depending on who's asked.
- fully established, de facto southern capital at [[Kirinar]]. [[Keepers]] hold heavy political influence here.
- Connects to [[Amaron Vale]] via Karog Pass, which goes through the [[Nahlia Mountains]]

## Midlands

**Borders:** N: [[Frigid Tundra]] | S: [[Southlands]], [[Westwood]] | E: [[Nahlia Mountains]] | W: [[Westwood]], western coast

- Once united under warrior-king [[Rhodan]]. His final war against the Southern Kingdoms ended in the [[Harrowing of the Midlands]] (57–59AK) — near-total destruction of the region's settlements and population.
- No large city or central authority has risen there since.
- Hardy, fiercely independent culture; unmatched fighting tradition — many of Laria's finest swordsmen are of Midland stock.
- Geographic crossroads of the continent — armies and migrants of all kinds cross Midland soil, invited or not.
- 495AK status: independent, no central authority (since ~59AK), warrior culture intact. Currently the transit corridor for [[Kirinar]]'s army marching north to [[Unukai]].

## Westwood

**Borders:** N: [[Midlands]] | S: [[Southlands]] | E: [[Midlands]], [[Southlands]] | W: western coast

- Old forest along Laria's southwestern coast. Never claimed by any kingdom.
- Home to scattered druidic enclaves and forest communities, including the reclusive [[Kitsune]] (Foxfolk).
- Has kept itself beyond the reach of southern law for as long as anyone can trace.
- Shares something of [[Tieryf Vale]]'s aloofness — content to be left alone, but historically willing to answer when a crisis is severe enough.
- unclaimed, uninvolved in the current southern/northern conflict.

---

## Nahlia Mountains

**Borders:** N: [[Frigid Tundra]] | S: [[Southlands]], [[Tieryf Vale]] | E: [[Amaron Vale]] | W: [[Midlands]]

- Home to [[Kaggrim]], the oldest continuously-inhabited city in Laria. Dwarven civilization here since the earliest Elder Era.
- Dominates the center of the continent. Its rivers feed both the [[Southlands]] and the [[Amaron Vale]] (via the Karog Pass).
- Smaller mountain settlements dot the range's valleys and lakes; none rival Kaggrim in size or significance.
- Was briefly, catastrophically overrun by demons after [[Ythria]]'s fall — the event that triggered the [[Great Exodus]] and reshaped the continent's demographics.
- [[Kaggrim]] standing, ancient, populous.

## Amaron Vale

**Borders:** N: [[Frigid Tundra]] | S: [[Tieryf Vale]] | E: [[Eastern Rim]] | W: [[Nahlia Mountains]]

- Settled in earnest during the [[Great Exodus]]. Named for Amrûn, its original Elvish name, reflecting light elven presence predating organized settlement.
- Founding population became the [[Eastern Kingdoms]]: [[Esterlin]] in the north and [[Korsa]] in the south.
- Hemmed by mountain ranges on both sides: the [[Nahlia Mountains]] to the west, the [[Eastern Rim]] to the east — the latter separating the Vale from the coast.
- Deliberately cosmopolitan — founding population was a melting pot of southern exiles, mountain-dwelling refugees, and elves.
- Settled since ~250AK, but currently recovering from the [[War of the Dead]] (488–491AK) — only 4–7 years past. Population reduced, infrastructure damaged, countryside fel-scarred.

## Frigid Tundra

**Borders:** N: [[Bitter Heights]], [[Zuriel Heights]], northern coast | S: [[Midlands]], [[Nahlia Mountains]] | E: [[Clanwoods]] | W: western coast

- Harsh, sparsely-forested expanse across the north-central continent.
- Settled by the largest wave of the [[Great Exodus]] — refugees fleeing the demon-haunted [[Nahlia Mountains]] and the [[Keepers]]' increasingly heavy hand in the south.
- [[Unukai]] rose here as the great northern city of its age — a haven for people the rest of Laria had little patience for.
- Thinly populated, independent-minded region overall; demands more of its people than the softer south.
- 495AK status: [[Unukai]] is a large, cosmopolitan clan-city, **currently under siege** by [[Kirinar]]'s army, on the verge of existential crisis.

---

## Bitter Heights

**Borders:** N: northern coast | S: [[Frigid Tundra]] | E: eastern coast | W: western coast

- A high plateau peninsula of arctic plains and forests, north of the [[Frigid Tundra]] — the harshest country in Laria.
- Separated from the [[Frigid Tundra]] by the Cliff Wall - the Bitter Heights is the plateau at the top of the cliff.
- Gave rise to [[Uldurok]].
- Home to the last surviving dragons, withdrawn into its deepest, least hospitable reaches and cave systems (see Peoples doc, Dragons) — territory even Uldurok's sorcerer-lords have not fully claimed.
- 495AK status: [[Uldurok]] is a mature, powerful, expansionist kingdom. King alive, sorcerers active.

---

## Clanwoods

**Borders:** N: [[Bitter Heights]], northern coast | S: [[Amaron Vale]] | E: eastern coast | W: [[Frigid Tundra]]

- Forested territory south/east of the Bitter Heights proper. Ancestral home of the [[Northern Clans]] since the earliest [[Great Exodus]] waves settled there.
- Dark magic took root here more thoroughly than anywhere else in Laria — first among Northern Clan shamans/sorcerer-lords, later in Uldurok itself.
- Settlements (Norcath among them) maintain independent clan identities/loyalties rather than centralized authority.
- 495AK status: independent clan network, currently the target of the "Keepers Go North" campaign (492AK) and resentful of it.

---

## Zuriel Peaks

**Borders:** N: [[Bitter Heights]], northern coast | S: [[Clanwoods]] | E: eastern coast | W: [[Frigid Tundra]]

- Rugged mountain range at the edge of the [[Clanwoods]] and [[Bitter Heights]].
- For most of Laria's history, unremarkable — home to nothing more than a small dwarven mining settlement.
- Riddled with old mining tunnels, sparsely populated beyond that settlement.
- home to [[Taigaal]], a tiny, ~32-year-old dwarven mining village (founded 463AK), unknown outside its own region, no non-dwarven residents on record.

---

## Tieryf Vale

**Borders:** N: [[Amaron Vale]] | S: southern coast | E: eastern coast | W: [[Southlands]]

- Ancient elven forest homeland (see Peoples doc, Elves).
- In the Elder Era, stretched unbroken from the [[Southlands]] to the eastern coast — an entire corner of the continent under elven stewardship.
- Nahrûn is a living, forested elven city.

---

## Eastern Rim

**Borders:** N: [[Clanwoods]] | S: [[Tieryf Vale]] | E: eastern coast | W: [[Amaron Vale]]

- a thin mountain range stretching along the middle of the eastern coast of Laria
- Mountain range separating the [[Amaron Vale]] from the eastern coast.
- [[Korsa]] lies at its southern reaches, nestled in the eaves of its final peaks
- temperate grasslands separate it and [[Tieryf Vale]] to the south

---

## Arkenthand

**Borders:** island! Sea on all sides

- Large island off Laria's eastern coast - narrow sea separates it from the [[Eastern Rim]] and [[Amaron Vale]]
- Sparsely settled, but no known towns or settlements
