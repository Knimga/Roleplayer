# Laria 5e Docs Index

Cross-reference convention (applies to every doc below): `[[Name]]` marks a
named place, faction, or individual with its own (or an expected) lookup
entry elsewhere among these docs. Used only where there's a real dependency
worth following — not for every mention of a generic term.

## regions.md
Covers: every named region of Laria — borders, key settlement(s), and
current (495AK) political/military status.
Search when: establishing where a scene or location sits geographically,
or checking a region's current-day status (independent, allied, at war,
under siege, etc.).

## history.md
Covers: the full timeline from the Elder Era through 494AK — founding
events, the Demonic War, the Great Exodus, the Arcane Laws, the War of the
Dead, and a list of unresolved threads left open for GM use.
Search when: a player references past events, asks "why is X the way it
is," or an NPC's motivation/backstory needs grounding in established
continuity.

## peoples.md
Covers: every playable and major non-player people (Elves, Dwarves,
Humans, Orcs, Gnomes, Kitsune, Halflings, Dragonborn, the six Warborn
subtypes, plus Dragons and Kobolds) — homeland, culture, governance, and
current posture toward the war.
Search when: a player character's race needs cultural grounding, or an
NPC/creature's people-specific traits or behavior matter to a scene.

## cities-and-towns.md
Covers: every named settlement — region, founding, ruler/governance, and
current (495AK) status.
Search when: the party arrives at, discusses, or asks about a specific
city or town not already established in the current scene.

## factions.md
Covers: the major political/military factions active in the current
conflict (Southern Kingdoms, the Keepers, Northern Clans, Uldurok, the
Shadowcult, the Elves of Tieryf Vale, Dragons) — motivations,
relationships, and current posture.
Search when: an NPC's allegiance matters, or a scene touches the ongoing
war's political dimension.
