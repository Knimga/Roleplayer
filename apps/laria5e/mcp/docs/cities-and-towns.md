## Kirinar

- Located in the [[Southlands]]. Began as a humble trading town; endured where dozens of rival Elder Era kingdoms fell.
- The largest city in Laria and the de facto capital of the [[Southern Kingdoms]].
- Birthplace of the [[Arcane Laws]] — the movement to codify magical restrictions began here and spread outward.
- The [[Keepers]] hold heavy political influence in the city.
- Kirinar's army is currently mobilized, having marched north through the [[Midlands]] to besiege [[Unukai]].

---

## Esterlin

- Located in the northern [[Amaron Vale]], near the [[Nahlia Mountains]], one of the region's two founding settlements and centers of power.
- Founded during the [[Great Exodus]] by southern migrants passing through the Karog Pass, alongside dwarves, gnomes, and elves.
- Ruled by King Tyrik, whose descent into dark ritual magic and transformation into a lich set off the [[War of the Dead]] against [[Korsa]].
- Currently recovering from that war: reduced population, damaged infrastructure, fel-scarred countryside nearby.

---

## Korsa

- Located in the southern [[Amaron Vale]], alongside [[Esterlin]] one of the region's two founding settlements and centers of power.
- Founded during the [[Great Exodus]], same origins as Esterlin.
- Ruled by King Nayo, who became a lich in the final stages of the [[War of the Dead]] rather than accept defeat by Esterlin.
- Currently recovering from that war, same as Esterlin: reduced population, damaged infrastructure, fel-scarred countryside nearby.

---

## Kaggrim

- Located at the southern tip of the [[Nahlia Mountains]], founded beside a natural spring at a valley's end.
- The oldest continuously-inhabited city in Laria — stood through every calamity in the continent's long history.
- The original and largest dwarven population center; the other two dwarven traditions ([[Amaron Vale]], [[Taigaal]]) trace back to migrations away from Kaggrim's people.
- Ancient, populous, standing.

---

## Taigaal

- Located on the southern eaves of the [[Zuriel Peaks]], on the border with the [[Clanwoods]], founded by dwarven settlers drawn by ore deposits and defensible stone.
- Tiny, obscure mining village. Unknown outside its own region; sits far from any trade route and outside both Kirinar's and Uldurok's notice.
- No non-dwarven residents on record.
- Home to a geomancer tradition — earth-shapers who deepen the settlement's mining tunnels — but the tradition is untested and unknown to the wider world.

---

## Norcath

- Located in the northern [[Clanwoods]], among several settlements there.
- Maintains its own clan identity and loyalties, in keeping with the region's decentralized structure — more akin to the old Northern Clan way of organizing than to any centralized kingdom.

---

## Unukai

- Located in the western [[Frigid Tundra]]. Grew from a small town into a great walled city, ruled by a council of clan leaders known as the Clanshead.
- The cosmopolitan hub of the north — its population a mix of every race and creed, including dark magic users.
- Cultural values: warrior tradition, anti-authoritarianism, shamanism, individual freedom, honor and fairness prized over hierarchy or bureaucracy.
- Higher crime rate and weaker economy than the Southern Kingdoms, but regarded by northmen as a symbol of resistance to southern and Keeper influence.
- Home to the **Black Bastille**, a prison built of magic-resistant stone, originally constructed to hold powerful members of the [[Shadowcult]].
- The [[Shadowcult]] operates secretly within the city, its members having been bound into worshipful service of [[Uldurok]]'s sorcerers, who appeared to them posing as dark gods.
- Currently under siege by [[Kirinar]]'s army, having just watched [[Uldurok]] reveal itself to the world.

---

## Thangara

- A coastal fortress-holding on the eastern coast of the [[Clanwoods]].
- Ruled by the sorcerer-king Gurthaar, a vassal of [[Uldurok]].
- Home to a large population of magically-bound slaves under Gurthaar's control.

---

## The Black Fortress

- Uldurok's seat of power, built at the northern tip of the [[Bitter Heights]] peninsula.
- Houses the king's court, the kingdom's nobility, and its schools of dark magic — the first such schools founded since [[Ythria]] itself, aimed at mastering dark power rather than merely studying it.
- Its deepest vaults hold Uldurok's own arsenal alongside a growing collection of studied [[Demonic War]] relics.

---

## Nahrûn

- A grand elven city at the eastern reach of [[Tieryf Vale]], part of the Vale's full, unbroken forest extent.
- Home to elves who, per the wider elven culture, remain watchful of the current war but uninvolved.

---

## Ythria

- A kingdom in the Nahlia Mountains founded to pursue magical study above all else.
- Site of the first discovery of dark magic and, later, summoning magic — the ritual that opened a rift to the material plane and unleashed the [[Demonic War]].
- Destroyed in that war's opening act. Now lies in ruins.
- Its tunnels once held the kingdom's darkest magical discoveries etched in stone and recorded in great black tomes; the fate and current location of that knowledge is not fully accounted for — some survived via fleeing Ythrian scholars, including those who later trained Uldurok's first sorcerers. **Unresolved thread — usable as a live hook, not a settled fact.**
