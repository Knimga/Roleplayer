## The Beginning (undated, pre-1AK)

- Earliest records are sparse, from fragmentary manuscripts. First peoples lived along the southwestern coast, expanded inward/northward.
- Early efforts centered on taming Laria's wilderness — no roads, no safe havens, dangerous creatures roamed freely.
- ~500 years after earliest records: first magic orders and temples founded. Elder Era magic is described as more easily summoned/manipulated than later eras.
- Dragons predate all of this — see Peoples doc, Dragons entry, for their Elder Era society and elf contact.

## 237UT — Elves Settle Tieryf Vale
*Elder Era founding myth. No UT→AK conversion exists; treat as ancient history regardless of exact gap.*
- Elves drawn to the forest that became [[Tieryf Vale]] — at this time, unbroken from the Southlands to the eastern coast.
- Elves lightly explored/settled parts of the present-day [[Amaron Vale]] during this era, ahead of any organized settlement there.

## 202UT — Dwarves Found Kaggrim
*Elder Era founding myth. Same UT caveat as above.*
- Dwarven migration follows a river from the [[Nahlia Mountains]] to its source at a natural spring.
- [[Kaggrim]] founded on that spot; becomes the oldest continuously-inhabited city in Laria.

## 1AK — The Seeds of Kirinar
- Southlands are politically volatile in this period — many small kingdoms rise and fall over several hundred years.
- [[Kirinar]], originally a humble trading town, outlasts its rivals and grows into the de facto provincial capital, eventually the largest city in Laria.

## 57–59AK — Rhodan and the Harrowing of the Midlands
- Warrior-king [[Rhodan]] unites the [[Midlands]], defends it from indigenous threats, rules for decades.
- In old age, grows arrogant; relations with the [[Southern Kingdoms]] deteriorate; Rhodan declares war on the more powerful south.
- Rhodan inflicts heavy casualties and holds out far longer than expected, but his armies are eventually destroyed.
- **The Harrowing:** the Southern Kingdoms raze nearly every settlement in the Midlands and kill much of the population in retaliation.
- Result: no large city or central authority has arisen in the Midlands since. Independent, hardy warrior culture persists.

## 73AK — Founding of Ythria and Discovery of Dark Magic
- Magic-focused kingdom founded in the mountains. [[Ythria]] is first to discover dark magic.
- At founding, "dark" magic is understood as a scholarly antipode to light magic (like fire to water) — not coded as evil.
- First great magical tomes written; first dedicated magic academies founded.

## 75–222AK — The Arcane Renaissance
- Magical knowledge from Ythria spreads rapidly through Laria; nearly every branch of magic is revolutionized.
- General attitude toward magic becomes liberal/unrestrained; all magic types explored to their fullest extent.
- Arcane universities spread through central and southern Laria.
- In Ythria specifically, a cadre of sorcerers and wizards pursues dark magic with the kingdom's full backing; discoveries recorded in tunnels and black tomes.
- At the height of this pursuit, Ythrian dark practitioners discover summoning magic.

### 207AK — The Arcane Laws
- Movement starts in [[Kirinar]], spreads through the southern realm, calling for stricter magical controls — reaction to the unrestrained Renaissance ethos.
- First version of the [[Arcane Laws]] outlaws:
  - Raising of undead (resurrection still permitted)
  - Overriding of free will (influence/suggestion still permitted)
- Laws are contentious; critics (notably Ythria) frame them as southern domination. Ythria publicly denounces them.

## 222–230AK — The Demonic War
- In [[Ythria]]'s tunnels, a group of powerful mages attempt a ritual; they lose control, tearing open a rift to the material plane and releasing fel energy and demon legions.
- Ythria is destroyed; demons spill outward across Laria.
- Demons move first against the dragons of the north (see Peoples doc, Dragons) in a deliberate campaign — by the time the war's famous battles occur, dragon society has already been shattered.
- A hasty alliance of central and southern kingdoms stems the demon advance; [[Elves]] of [[Tieryf Vale]] join the fight, allowing the alliance to push demons back.
- The greatest magisters of elf and man close the rift, giving their own lives to do so.
- Demons are scattered, not eradicated — survivors flee into hiding across Laria's corners.
- Demonic War relics (weapons, armor, instruments) are left scattered across the continent where combatants fell — no central recovery, no full accounting of what was found vs. lost. **Unresolved thread, see bottom of doc.**

## 231AK — Formation of the Keepers
- Formed in response to the Arcane Renaissance's excesses and the Ythria disaster.
- An order of learned mages and clerics, many of whom helped write the original Arcane Laws.
- Holds significant influence with [[Kirinar]]'s government from its founding.
- Two new prohibitions added to the Arcane Laws under Keeper influence:
  - Planar magic (opening portals to other planes)
  - Demonic magic (summoning/commanding demons)
- Keeper ranks swell; agents spread through southern/middle Laria hunting demons and dark magic users.

## 232–243AK — The Rebuilding
- South rebuilds after the Demonic War; stray demons still roam the countryside during this period.
- Arcane Laws become deeply embedded in southern culture; some communities interpret them more broadly than written (e.g., blanket suspicion of necromancy or enchantment schools regardless of specific spell effects).
- Cultural intensity of Arcane Law enforcement decreases moving north; largely ignored in the far north.
- Keepers concentrate their demon/dark-mage hunting in the north during this period; their relative absence in the south leads to the rise of independent demon-hunting guilds there.
- Growth in paladins, clerics, temples to good-aligned gods in the south.
- In the north, [[Unukai]] grows from a town into a true city — the only one in the north at this time.
- Surviving dragons withdraw fully into the [[Bitter Heights]] during this period, taking up residence among [[Kobolds]] (see Peoples doc).

## 247–262AK — The Great Exodus
- Demonic presence lingers in middle Laria for years after the war's end; the region remains dangerous.
- Keepers continue hunting remaining demons/dark mages during this period but gain a reputation for extreme methods — civilian deaths, property destruction, no accountability to the populace given their status with Kirinar's government.
- Combination of ongoing demon threat + Keeper overreach drives mass migration out of southern Laria and the Midlands.
- Migration routes:
  - Through the [[Karog Pass]] into the [[Amaron Vale]] → becomes the founding population of the [[Eastern Kingdoms]].
  - Northward through the Midlands into the [[Frigid Tundra]] and [[Clanwoods]].
  - A further subset pushes past that into the [[Bitter Heights]], scaling the Cliff Wall — driven by a desire to maximize distance from the south. Renegade Ythrian mages are drawn to and influence this group specifically.
- Keepers harass migrant trains along the way; general distrust of Keepers among migrants leads them to shelter renegade mages from Keeper attention.
- Dark magic becomes the dominant magical practice among the Bitter Heights migrants — direct seed for [[Uldurok]]'s founding.

### 250AK — Settling of Amaron Vale
- First large wave of [[Great Exodus]] migrants settle the region — mostly southerners via the Karog Pass, plus dwarves, gnomes, and other mountain-dwelling peoples fleeing the demon-infested [[Nahlia Mountains]].
- [[Esterlin]] and [[Korsa]] founded among the first settlements; become the region's two centers of power.
- Elven influence present from the outset (place names, positive relations, elven citizens/leaders) due to earlier light elven settlement of the region.

### 256AK — Formation of the Northern Clans
- Great Exodus exiles in the north develop a distinct barbarian culture, splitting into two main groups: those in the wider Frigid Tundra/Clanwoods region, and those higher in the [[Bitter Heights]].
- Both groups are led/protected by dark magic practitioners from the outset.
- The wider Frigid Tundra/Clanwoods group forms into an interlocking network known as the [[Northern Clans]].

### 258AK — Founding of Uldurok
- Bitter Heights sorcerer-lords (mostly of Ythrian descent) establish enclaves; unify local tribes through conquest.
- Deep-seated hatred of the south present among Bitter Heights peoples from this founding period.
- Bands entering the fold bring Demonic War relics with them, treated initially as trophies/curiosities; Uldurok's sorcerers recognize their value and begin serious study — relics stored in the fortress's deepest vaults alongside Uldurok's own arsenal.
- [[Uldurok]] founded as a true state: king, nobility, laws, standing army — named after an Ythrian hero.
- First schools of dark magic since Ythria itself are founded in Uldurok's towers, aimed at mastering power rather than merely studying it.
- First century of Uldurok's existence spent gathering resources, training armies, consolidating power.

## 282–487AK — Relative Peace
- Kingdoms of Laria at relative peace with each other during this stretch; the north is regarded as unruly but not openly hostile.
- [[Eastern Kingdoms]] grow in wealth and stature to match the western kingdoms.
- Keepers repeatedly warn during this period of Uldurok's growing threat.

## 307AK — The Ascent of Unukai
- [[Unukai]] grows from a town into a great walled city, governed by a council of clan leaders (the Clanshead).
- Becomes the cosmopolitan hub of the north — mixed population, culture built on warrior values, anti-authoritarianism, shamanism, and tolerance of unrestrained magical pursuit.
- High crime rate, weaker economy than the Southern Kingdoms, but regarded as a symbol of northern resistance to southern/Keeper influence.
- Clanshead tolerates dark magic practice within the city (officially denied to placate the Keepers).
- Some Unukai mages achieve enough skill to open planar portals — in doing so, they are observed (unknowingly) by [[Uldurok]]'s sorcerers.
- Ulduroki sorcerers, trained by surviving Ythrian elves and led by [[Ildrazza]], are far more powerful than the Unukai mages they observe. They reveal themselves posing as dark gods and magically bind the Unukai mages' will into worshipful servitude.
- This event is the founding of the [[Shadowcult]] — active within Unukai from this point forward, secretly serving Uldurok.

## 325AK — The Growing Shadow of Uldurok
- Uldurok's long-standing desire for revenge against the south persists and matures into active planning: resource gathering, army training, fortress strengthening, siege-weapon construction — all conducted while keeping the southern border and its Keepers blind to the buildup.
- [[Unukai]] stands as an independent northern power that stands in the way of any southward conquest — a haven for all races/creeds including dark magic users, officially outlawing dark magic only for appearances toward the Keepers.

## 463AK — Founding of Taigaal
- Dwarven settlers push into the [[Zuriel Peaks]], drawn by ore deposits and defensible stone — same founding instinct as [[Kaggrim]] generations earlier.
- Site chosen is far from both Kirinar's and Uldurok's notice, with no trade route nearby.
- Settlement named [[Taigaal]]; grows slowly and quietly. Its geomancers deepen tunnels for ore, not for any wider purpose.
- A small, unknown village outside its own region.

## 488–491AK — The War of the Dead
- [[Esterlin]] and [[Korsa]] had coexisted peacefully for centuries; new kings in each city (Tyrik of Esterlin, Nayo of Korsa) fall into a prideful feud that escalates through embargoes, skirmishes, and finally war.
- As Korsa gains the upper hand, King Tyrik turns to dark ritual magic in his instability, becoming an immortal lich, and raises undead armies against Korsa.
- Under siege, King Nayo of Korsa follows the same path, also becoming a lich, rather than accept defeat.
- The two lich-kings and their undead armies devastate the [[Amaron Vale]] as a battleground.
- The [[Keepers]], weakened by ~200 years of relative peace, cannot confront the liches directly — limited to sabotage, guerrilla resistance, and civilian evacuation.
- War ends when a lone paladin sacrifices his life to a god, temporarily banishing both liches and their armies.
- Result: population of the Amaron Vale significantly reduced, infrastructure damaged, countryside fel-scarred.

## 492AK — The Keepers Go North
- In the war's aftermath, the south learns Tyrik's lich-transformation was enabled by a black tome sourced from the [[Clanwoods]].
- [[Kirinar]] commissions the [[Keepers]] to enter the north and locate/destroy any remaining sources of dark magical knowledge tied to the black tomes.
- Keeper conduct in the north (extrajudicial killings, property destruction) rapidly earns the hatred of the northern peoples, who hold a strong sense of independence and sovereignty.
- News of Keeper abuses reaches the Clanshead at [[Unukai]] (the north's unofficial leading authority); Unukai deploys forces to obstruct the Keepers, authorizing lethal resistance.
- Several independent barbarian tribes take vigilante action against Keepers as well.
- Kirinar responds to reports of Keeper deaths by increasing Keeper presence and funding in the north.
- Tension escalates as Keepers develop more aggressive infiltration methods.
- Unukai's [[Black Bastille]] (a magic-resistant prison originally built to hold powerful Shadowcult members) fills with captured Keepers during this period.
- Keepers confirm the presence of significant dark-mage activity and planar-magic research within Unukai's walls — this discovery is what drives Kirinar's response.

## 494AK — Kirinar and Unukai
- On confirmation of planar magic practice within [[Unukai]], [[Kirinar]] mobilizes its armies northward.
- The march through the [[Midlands]] takes roughly a year (vs. a few months in peacetime) due to persistent Northern Clan guerrilla resistance — lightning raids, dark-mage/shaman tactics unfamiliar to southern forces.
- Despite this resistance, the southern army's numbers, equipment, and funding allow steady, if slow, progress north.
- On arrival in the [[Frigid Tundra]], Kirinar's forces lay siege to Unukai.
- Uldurok reveals itself to the world and lays claim to all of Laria.
- Unukai stands, under active siege by Kirinar's army.

## Unresolved Threads (live hooks, not settled facts)

- **Demonic War relics:** recovery after 222–230AK was slow and incomplete; many relics remain unaccounted for in the wild, especially in the north.
- **Nahlia Mountains demon infestation:** left open to DM discretion.
- **Ildrazza and the Ulduroki sorcerers:** active antagonists; their long-term fate is not something the party has any way to know.
