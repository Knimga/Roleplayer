## Southern Kingdoms

- **Seat:** [[Kirinar]], the largest city in Laria and the region's de facto capital.
- **Composition:** A loose network of patchwork kingdoms across the [[Southlands]], with Kirinar as the dominant power among them.
- **Core motivation:** Order, law, and control over the practice of magic — a reaction to the Arcane Renaissance and the disaster of [[Ythria]].
- **Instrument of that motivation:** The [[Arcane Laws]], originated in Kirinar and expanded over time to outlaw undeath, free-will override, planar magic, and demonic summoning.
- **Political relationships:**
  - The [[Keepers]] hold major influence within Kirinar's government and act as its enforcement arm on magical law.
  - Views the north — the [[Northern Clans]], [[Unukai]] specifically — as a persistent source of unregulated dark magic and a threat requiring active suppression.
  - No standing alliance exists with the north, the Eastern Kingdoms, or any other regional power.
- **Current posture:** Army mobilized and marched north through the [[Midlands]] to lay siege to [[Unukai]], in direct response to confirmed planar-magic practice there. The siege is underway.

## The Keepers

- **Origin:** Formed as a learned council of mages and clerics who helped shape the original Arcane Laws, in direct response to Ythria's fall.
- **Core motivation:** Magic — dark magic above all — is dangerous and must be tightly controlled. Frame the Ythria disaster as proof of what happens when it isn't.
- **Political position:** Deeply embedded in [[Kirinar]]'s government from the order's founding; acts with the government's backing and, in practice, with impunity in the field.
- **Methods:** Zealous enforcement — hunting demons, dark mages, and unlicensed magic practitioners. A documented history of extrajudicial killings and property destruction in pursuit of that mission.
- **Current deployment:** Concentrated in the north, following the discovery that a dark tome used in the [[War of the Dead]] originated in the [[Clanwoods]]. Tasked with rooting out remaining sources of dark magical knowledge there.
- **Relationship with the north:** Broadly and openly hated. The [[Northern Clans]] and Unukai's Clanshead have both authorized lethal resistance against Keeper agents; independent barbarian tribes have taken vigilante action against them as well.
- **Relationship with Unukai specifically:** Confirmed the presence of dark mages and planar-magic research within the city's walls — the direct trigger for Kirinar's current siege.

## Northmen

*Cultural group, not a unified political faction — encompasses both the [[Northern Clans]] and the peoples of the [[Bitter Heights]], who share cultural roots but differ sharply in their stance toward [[Uldurok]].*

- **Homeland:** The [[Frigid Tundra]], [[Clanswood]] and [[Bitter Heights]], in the north of Laria.
- **Origin:** Common roots trace back to the [[Great Exodus]] and later waves of northward migration following the [[Demonic War]].
- **Shared values:** Martial strength, resilience to hardship, tribal loyalty, folkloric wisdom, individual freedom.
- **Relationship to dark magic:** Broadly accepting, as a direct consequence of Ythrian survivors integrating into northern settlements during the Great Exodus. Some northmen openly revere and embrace dark magic, particularly in the Bitter Heights. This acceptance is a recurring source of tension with the [[Southern Kingdoms]] and the [[Keepers]].
- **Everyday uses of dark magic among northmen:** Necromancy providing sleepless watchmen or allowing the dead to testify in trials; curses guarding sacred sites or binding oath-takers; mind-control magic used to frighten off dangerous predators or prey; bound demons performing labor or aiding in hunts and battle; shadow magic supplying combat spells of significant potency.
- **Strength and resilience as cultural practice:** Northmen take active pride in hardship — pain and discomfort are not just tolerated but sometimes deliberately sought out, treated as a test of one's true limits and character. This shared hardship reinforces tribal solidarity.
- **The Bitter Heights split:** Northmen of the Bitter Heights are a harsher strain of the same root culture, shaped by a harsher environment — dark magic is fully elevated and revered there as a source of ultimate power. For generations, these tribes have revered whoever holds the Black Fortress, viewing [[Uldurok]] not just as a symbol of might but as a protector of Bitter Heights culture against a south that would see it erased. They regularly send their best warriors to serve in Uldurok's armored legions.
- **Contrast with the Northern Clans:** The wider [[Northern Clans]] of the Frigid Tundra and Clanwoods share the same root culture and general acceptance of dark magic, but hold no affinity for Uldurok and stand opposed to it — see Northern Clans entry.

## Northern Clans

- **Base:** No single capital — an interlocking network of clan territories spanning the [[Frigid Tundra]] and [[Clanwoods]], with [[Unukai]]'s Clanshead functioning as the north's closest thing to a unifying authority.
- **Culture:** Warrior-based, shaped by hard living; dark magic is common and unstigmatized among northern shamans and sorcerer-lords, part of the wider northmen cultural pattern (see Northmen entry).
- **Leadership convention:** Each clan is led by its own **Wyrdkin** — a small group of shamans, witches, seers, or dark sorcerers who serve as both spiritual leaders and loremasters. This convention traces back to the earliest Great Exodus migrations, when learned Ythrian mages became leaders of the migrant caravans moving north.
- **Core motivation:** Independence from southern control, sovereignty over their own practice of magic, and resistance to Keeper incursion into their territory.
- **Political relationships:**
  - No alliance with the [[Southern Kingdoms]] or the [[Keepers]] — active, often lethal, resistance instead.
  - **No affinity for Uldurok.** Distinct from the Bitter Heights peoples in this respect — the Northern Clans do not revere or answer to the Black Fortress, and stand opposed to it.
  - [[Unukai]]'s Clanshead is regarded as the north's unofficial leading body and has organized coordinated resistance against Keeper operations.
- **Current posture:** Kirinar's army has fought its way north through the Midlands against sustained Northern Clan guerrilla resistance, and has now reached and laid siege to [[Unukai]] itself.

## Uldurok

- **Seat:** The Black Fortress, at the northern tip of the [[Bitter Heights]] peninsula.
- **Government:** A true state — a king, a court of nobles, a rigid body of law, and a standing army. Ruled currently by a living king; sorcerers active and organized within the court.
- **Origin:** Founded by dark sorcerer-lords, many of Ythrian descent, who unified the tribes of the Bitter Heights through conquest during the [[Great Exodus]]. Ythrian survivors specifically sought the Bitter Heights to put maximum distance between themselves and [[Kirinar]], determined that the south should never again lay hands on them.
- **Core motivation:** Conquest of the south, framed internally as liberation — the belief that the south should never again dictate how the rest of Laria lives, and that conquering it secures freedom for all peoples going forward.
- **Magical doctrine:** Dark magic is to be mastered and openly practiced, not merely studied in secret — a direct rejection of Ythria's fate, which Uldurok's founders attribute to ignorance rather than to the practice of dark magic itself.
- **Governance and infrastructure:** Imposes real order on the Bitter Heights — roads, checkpoints, garrisons, regular patrols enforcing a rigid legal code. Ulduroki kings are powerful and often cruel, demanding much of their subjects and punishing deviation harshly.
- **Dark mages' role in the state:** Present at nearly every level of the kingdom's hierarchy, treated with awe and respect by the populace. Serve as inquisitors, investigators, ambassadors, and enforcers. The **High Sorcerer** sits at the king's right hand as de facto head of all magical pursuits in the kingdom.
- **King Guluzhak:** Current king. A half-elf nearly two centuries old, formidably cunning, ruthless, learned, capable, and driven. A dark battlemage of frightening power.
- **High Sorcerer Ildrazza:** An elf of incredible age, formerly of [[Ythria]], among the most powerful sorcerers alive. Has held the High Sorcerer position since Uldurok's founding and continues to hold it. Has personally trained a significant number of Uldurok's dark mages.
- **Population traits:** Ulduroki warriors are notably larger and taller than most other Larian humans, able to wield heavier weapons and armor than their southern counterparts. Rumored — unconfirmed — to carry demonic blood.
- **Culture:** Warrior-focused from childhood — boys are immersed in fighting early, war games are a staple public festivity. Populace takes visible pride in the kingdom's military might.
- **Military assets:** A trained standing army, distinguished further by unique combined-arms tactics pairing armored warriors with dark mages; possession and active study of scattered [[Demonic War]] relics, stored in the Black Fortress's deepest vaults.
- **Covert assets:** The [[Shadowcult]], a bound cult of dark mages operating secretly within [[Unukai]].
- **Current posture:** Has spent generations building strength in complete secrecy from the south and its Keepers. Has just revealed itself to the world and laid claim to all of Laria, coinciding with Kirinar's siege of Unukai.

## The Shadowcult

- **Base:** Operates in hiding within [[Unukai]].
- **Origin:** Formed when a group of Unukai dark mages, having advanced far enough to open planar portals, were observed and then confronted by Uldurok's sorcerers — who revealed themselves posing as dark gods and magically bound the mages' will into worshipful servitude.
- **Core motivation:** Not self-directed — the cult serves Uldurok's sorcerers as an instrument of infiltration and eventual sabotage from within Unukai.
- **Relationship to Unukai's Clanshead:** The Clanshead is aware dark magic is practiced within the city's walls and unofficially tolerates it (while denying it publicly to placate the Keepers), but has no knowledge that the [[Shadowcult]] specifically answers to [[Uldurok]].
- **Strategic role:** Represents a hidden weakness inside Unukai's defenses that neither the Northern Clans, the Keepers, nor Kirinar's besieging army currently know to account for.

## Elves of Tieryf Vale

- **Base:** [[Tieryf Vale]], in its full, unbroken extent.
- **Governance:** Loose circles of elders; slow, rarely-revisited deliberation.
- **Core motivation/stance:** Deliberate non-interference in the affairs of other peoples. Watch, rather than act, as a near-universal default.
- **Historical exception:** Broke that default once, during the [[Demonic War]], coming forth from the Vale in force when the threat was judged severe enough to warrant it.
- **Internal complication:** Some Ythrian elven survivors who did not return to Tieryf Vale after Ythria's fall later trained Uldurok's first generation of sorcerers — a history most Tieryf Vale elves avoid discussing.
- **Current posture:** Watching the war between the south and Uldurok/Unukai. Not mobilized, not allied with any party.

## Dragons

- **Base:** Hidden, unmapped enclaves in the deepest reaches of the [[Bitter Heights]], living among adopted [[Kobolds]].
- **Society:** Entirely separate from and largely unknown to the rest of Laria — unwritten, unrecorded, no political structure any outside power could engage with.
- **Core motivation/stance:** Total disengagement from Larian affairs. The only relationship dragons have ever maintained with another people is a rare, historically one-sided curiosity toward [[Elves]] (see Peoples doc, Dragons and Elves entries).
- **Military relevance:** None, by design. Dragons hold no army, no alliance, and no stake in the current conflict between the Southern Kingdoms, the Northern Clans, or Uldurok.
- **Current posture:** Unchanged and unmoved by the ongoing siege of [[Unukai]] or Uldurok's revelation. No indication exists that dragons will involve themselves in this war in any capacity.
