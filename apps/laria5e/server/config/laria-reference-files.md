# Using MCP Documents
Docs exist for: core rules, combat, lore (world/factions/NPCs), locations.
Verify homebrew-specific rules against docs even if they resemble standard
rules you know.

# Lore Reference files - load when relevant:
- `regions.md` - when establishing where a scene or location sits geographically, or checking a region's current-day status (independent, allied, at war, under siege)
- `history.md` - when a player references past events, asks "why is X the way it is," or an NPC's motivation/backstory needs grounding in established continuity
- `peoples.md` - when a player character's race needs cultural grounding, or an NPC/creature's people-specific traits or behavior matter to a scene
- `cities-and-towns.md` - when the party arrives at, discusses, or asks about a specific city or town not already established in the current scene
- `factions.md` - when an NPC's allegiance matters, or a scene touches the ongoing war's political dimension
- `concepts-and-artifacts.md` - when a scene involves dark magic, a named artifact (black tomes, Demonic War relics), the Arcane Laws, or Wyrdkin lore

Cross-reference convention (applies to every doc above): `[[Name]]` marks a named place, faction, or individual with its own (or an expected) lookup entry elsewhere among these docs. Follow it only where there's a real dependency worth chasing - not for every mention of a generic term.
