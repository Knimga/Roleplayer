# Backstory: Desmond "Null" Lorentz

Desmond was born into Corporate comfort in New Westbrook, back when the district still had some of its old shine. His family were well connected Corporate Managers at Arasaka, the kind of people who had doors opened for them just by their name. He used those family ties to get into a junior security role at Arasaka's City Center offices, legitimate work, on the books, the kind of stable future his name was supposed to guarantee. 
It wasn't stable for long. Somewhere in that job he accessed something he was never meant to see, internal chatter about an unnamed "cleanup contract," a black budget line with nothing attached to it. He didn't understand what he'd stumbled onto, only he was not supposed to see it, which led to his family being killed in what looked, from the outside, like a random drone strike. The kind of tragedy Night City never bothers investigating.
He was the only survivor. In the wreckage, before anyone else showed up, he found a shard. Small enough to close a fist around, the casing scorched and cracked. He's carried it every day since, through his Solo years and into his life now as a Fixer. He's tried, quietly and carefully, to have it read more than once. Every attempt came back the same. Corrupted. As far as Desmond knows, whatever answer it once held died with his family.
With no Corporate backing left and a target he couldn't name, Desmond survived the only way he knew how. Solo work. Hired muscle, wetwork, anything that paid and kept him breathing. An aging Solo named Sable took him on, not out of charity, but because the kid had nerve and nothing left to lose. She trained him properly. How to read a room before it went loud. How to survive a Facedown instead of just winning a firefight. For a while she was the closest thing to the family he had left. Then she vanished. No warning, no goodbye, no explanation. He never found out why.
He came out the other side cold, patient, and certain of one thing. The people who did this couldn't be reached with a gun. They had to be out maneuvered. So he set violence aside as a first option and became a Fixer instead, carrying the old skills forward as a threat that's always loaded, never the plan. The shard stays with him always, a seemingly dead end he's never been able to let go of.

A few months ago, that changed. Vidik, a Tech he'd hired for his hands and his nerve, saw the shard and went quiet in a way Desmond had never seen from him. Vidik knew unmarked, off-book hardware when he saw it, the kind he used to move without asking questions back when asking questions got people hurt. He couldn't say what it was connected to. But for the first time in years, Desmond wasn't the only one who thought the dead end might not be so dead after all.
