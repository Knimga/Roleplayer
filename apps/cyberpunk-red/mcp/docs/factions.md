# Factions

Powers, Corps, Gangs, and Groups (2045)

## Scope Note

- Worldwide factions operate across nations or regions: the NeoCorps, Nomad Nations, and global NET/space powers.
- Night City factions are local: street gangs, local Nomad packs, and the city's own power blocs.
- Many NeoCorps maintain a branch in Night City, but the corporations themselves are national/international in scope.

## Worldwide Factions

### The NeoCorps

The 4th Corporate War ended the age of megacorp dominance. Survivors shrank into regional "Corpos" — closer to mid-20th-century companies than the world-spanning giants of the 2020s. They are smaller but more rapacious: fewer rules, covert ops squads, and bribery in place of standing armies. The recognized major players:

### Nomad Nations

Nomads run the world's trade, shipping, and open roads in the Time of the Red, using surplus military hardware to clear gogangs and make travel possible. Structure: a Blood Family numbers in the hundreds; an Affiliated Family is several Blood Families combined.

Major Nations:

- Aldecaldos: leading Reclaimers; heavily involved in rebuilding Night City.
- Jodes: originally farmers from the American Midwest.
- Blood Nation: specialize in traveling entertainment.
- Meta: formed from military personnel abandoned during the SouthAm Wars.

### Global NET and Space Powers

- Netwatch: NET-policing agency founded by a U.S./EuroTheatre treaty. With the NET dead, it now works as "lifeguards," pulling stray Netrunners out of the lethal Old NET (the Dungeon). Head: Magnificent Curtis.
- Transcendental AIs and the Ghosts: powerful, inscrutable machine intelligences in the dead NET. Alt Cunningham and her Ghosts act as go-betweens. Together with Netwatch they run the secret Black Wall project to contain what lives in the Old NET.
- The Highriders: space-dwellers independent since the Seven-Hour War. Control key orbital and lunar assets (O'Neill Two, Crystal Palace, mass drivers) beyond any Earth government's reach.

## Night City Factions

### Governance: The Night City Council

No central government and no true mayor. The city is run loosely by a junta of City Managers drawn from each district. Dominant power blocs on the Council:

- The Nomad Families (esp. the Aldecaldos)
- The Edgerunners (Solos, Netrunners, Rockerboys, and other cyberpunks)
- The old City government
- Surviving Corporate interests: Biotechnica, Continental Brands, Danger Gal, Militech, Network 54, Petrochem, Rocklin Augmentics, SovOil, Trauma Team, Ziggurat, Zhirafa, and (covertly) Arasaka.

No single faction dominates; disputes are often settled on The Street with weapons. Underfunded but heavily armed Police, Fire, and EMS operate citywide, paid by a levy on Corps and neighborhoods.

### Local Nomad Presence

- Aldecaldo Camp: large multicultural encampment at the city's edge; the Aldecaldos' trade, transport, and Badlands-guide hub, and their main point of business in Night City.
- Steel Vaqueros: a Nomad pack out of Salinas running the coastal highway from the Santa Cruz agriplots to the southern cities; supply deals with several Night City Reclaimer groups.

### Note on Corporate Presence

Most major NeoCorps keep a Night City branch (Militech at the NorCal base, Petrochem and SovOil across the street from each other, Biotechnica's dome campus, Raven Microcyb, Network 54, Zhirafa, Trauma Team Tower, etc.). Ziggurat and Danger Gal are headquartered in the city itself.
