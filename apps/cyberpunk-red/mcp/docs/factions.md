# FACTIONS
Powers, Corps, Gangs, and Groups (2045)

# SCOPE NOTE
- Worldwide factions operate across nations or regions: the NeoCorps, Nomad Nations, and global NET/space powers.
- Night City factions are local: street gangs, local Nomad packs, and the city's own power blocs.
- Many NeoCorps maintain a branch in Night City, but the corporations themselves are national/international in scope.

# ================================
# WORLDWIDE FACTIONS
# ================================

# THE NEOCORPS
The 4th Corporate War ended the age of megacorp dominance. Survivors shrank into regional "Corpos" — closer to mid-20th-century companies than the world-spanning giants of the 2020s. They are smaller but more rapacious: fewer rules, covert ops squads, and bribery in place of standing armies. The recognized major players:

- Corp: Arasaka
  Business: Corporate security, banking, manufacturing
  HQ: Tokyo, Japan
  Leader: Saburo Arasaka (centenarian patriarch); split into three factions
  Notes: Instigator of the 4th Corp War; banished from the continental U.S. and reduced to Japan-only for a decade. Now operates covertly in Night City. Fractured into:
    - Kiji (Green Pheasant): mainline regime, figurehead Hanako Arasaka; seeks reconciliation with the New U.S.
    - Taka (Hawk): renegade Yorinobu Arasaka; opposes the other factions.
    - Hato (Dove): U.S.-aligned, figurehead Michiko Sanderson (née Arasaka).

- Corp: Militech International
  Business: Arms manufacturing/distribution, mercenaries
  HQ: Washington D.C.
  Leader: Gen. Donald Lundee, USMC (ret.)
  Employees: 350,000 (plus 700,000 contracted to the U.S. military)
  Notes: Arasaka's great rival; fought it to a standstill. Nationalized by the New U.S., but secretly rebuilding as an arms merchant and mercenary army. Still the most militarily powerful corp in the world.

- Corp: Petrochem
  Business: Petrochemicals and agribusiness; world's largest CHOOH2 producer
  HQ: Dallas, Free State of Texas
  Leader: Angus Youngblood
  Employees: 338,000
  Notes: Keeps the world running on synthetic fuel. Controls vast farmland. Bitter rival of SovOil.

- Corp: SovOil
  Business: Petrochemicals and agribusiness (real oil)
  HQ: Moscow, Russia
  Leader: the Central Committee (membership unknown)
  Employees: 245,000
  Notes: Sits on the world's largest remaining oil reserves; dominant economic force in the Neo-Soviet Union. Diversifying before oil runs dry. Long-running feud with Petrochem.

- Corp: Biotechnica
  Business: Genetic engineering, microbiology, biochemistry
  HQ: La Jolla, California
  Leader: Nicolo Loggagia
  Employees: ~36,000
  Notes: Invented CHOOH2, the fuel that powers the world. Runs the "Reference Forest" project and experimental bio-engineered flora and fauna.

- Corp: Trauma Team International
  Business: Ambulance and paramedic services
  HQ: Seattle, Washington
  Leaders: Carrie Lachanan and Bob Jones
  Employees: 15,000
  Notes: Elite armed medical response by AV. Now regional and overstretched. Responds to paying clients; Edgerunners pool funds for a single call card.

- Corp: Network 54
  Business: Nationwide broadcasting
  HQ: Santa Fe, New Mexico
  Leader: Michelle Dreyer
  Employees: 62,000
  Notes: Broadcasts on Channel 54 nationwide. Reach curtailed since the War. Mixes news, telenovelas, reality shows, and propaganda.

- Corp: Continental Brands
  Business: Organic and synthetic food and drink
  HQ: Tulsa, Oklahoma
  Leader: Olivia Forsythe ("the Kibble Queen")
  Employees: 147,000
  Notes: Former Petrochem food subsidiary. Runs loyalty-scored company communities ("Oasis") with rewards and punishments tied to kibble consumption.

- Corp: Zhirafa Technical Manufacturing
  Business: Drones, autonomous robotics, construction mecha
  HQ: Vladivostok, Russia
  Leader: Artyom Sokolov
  Employees: 225,000
  Notes: Rose fast in 2039 on the cheap, durable GRAF3 construction robot. Its drones patrol much of Night City. Murky origins.

- Corp: Ziggurat
  Business: Communications and data infrastructure
  HQ: Night City
  Leader: UR (pronounced "You Are")
  Employees: 115,000
  Notes: Appeared in 2030; built the CitiNets and Data Pools that replaced the dead NET across North America. Monetizes data, ads, and its "Garden" content platform. Founder hints at a personal link to Rache Bartmoss.

- Corp: Danger Gal
  Business: Private investigation and security
  HQ: Night City
  Leader: Michiko Sanderson (née Arasaka)
  Employees: 1,800
  Notes: High-society detective/security firm; secretly funded by President Kress and backed by inherited Arasaka databases. Michiko's bodyguard, Kenichi Zaburo, once fought Morgan Blackhand to a draw.

- Corp: Rocklin Augmentics
  Business: Cybernetics and cyberlimbs
  Leader: Jacinda Hidalgo ("The War Orphan")
  Notes: Aggressive post-War cybernetics maker that knocked Raven Microcyb down a peg. Hidalgo's own body is a walking product display.

# SECOND-TIER CORPS (brief)
- Constitution Arms: heavy weaponry manufacturer.
- GunMart: low-quality weapons.
- Kendachi Arms: Japanese arms; specializes in mono-molecular blades.
- Kiroshi Optics: dominant cybereye/optics maker.
- Merrill, Asukaga & Finch (MA&F): exclusive investment/financial firm; helped found Night City.
- Raven Microcybernetics: once-powerful cybernetics corp, diminished by Rocklin.
- Thornton Motor Company: durable mid-tier ground vehicles.
- WorldSat CommNet: controls real-time city-to-city A/V communication.
- Zetatech: software and heavy machinery.

# NOMAD NATIONS
Nomads run the world's trade, shipping, and open roads in the Time of the Red, using surplus military hardware to clear gogangs and make travel possible. Structure: a Blood Family numbers in the hundreds; an Affiliated Family is several Blood Families combined. Major Nations:
- Aldecaldos: leading Reclaimers; heavily involved in rebuilding Night City.
- Jodes: originally farmers from the American Midwest.
- Blood Nation: specialize in traveling entertainment.
- Meta: formed from military personnel abandoned during the SouthAm Wars.

# GLOBAL NET AND SPACE POWERS
- Netwatch: NET-policing agency founded by a U.S./EuroTheatre treaty. With the NET dead, it now works as "lifeguards," pulling stray Netrunners out of the lethal Old NET (the Dungeon). Head: Magnificent Curtis.
- Transcendental AIs and the Ghosts: powerful, inscrutable machine intelligences in the dead NET. Alt Cunningham and her Ghosts act as go-betweens. Together with Netwatch they run the secret Black Wall project to contain what lives in the Old NET.
- The Highriders: space-dwellers independent since the Seven-Hour War. Control key orbital and lunar assets (O'Neill Two, Crystal Palace, mass drivers) beyond any Earth government's reach.

# NIGHT CITY FACTIONS
# ================================

# GOVERNANCE: THE NIGHT CITY COUNCIL
No central government and no true mayor. The city is run loosely by a junta of City Managers drawn from each district. Dominant power blocs on the Council:
- The Nomad Families (esp. the Aldecaldos)
- The Edgerunners (Solos, Netrunners, Rockerboys, and other cyberpunks)
- The old City government
- Surviving Corporate interests: Biotechnica, Continental Brands, Danger Gal, Militech, Network 54, Petrochem, Rocklin Augmentics, SovOil, Trauma Team, Ziggurat, Zhirafa, and (covertly) Arasaka.
No single faction dominates; disputes are often settled on The Street with weapons. Underfunded but heavily armed Police, Fire, and EMS operate citywide, paid by a levy on Corps and neighborhoods.

# NIGHT CITY GANGS
The gangs rule the city's night. Entries below give style and turf.

- Gang: 6th Street
  Type: Vigilante / veteran gang
  Notes: Founded by 4th Corp War vets; well-armed and heavily cybered. Protects neighborhoods (notably the Holy Angels Church) but has turned to extortion and smuggling to survive.

- Gang: Maelstrom
  Type: Combat gang / cyberpsycho-adjacent
  Notes: Formed from the old Metal Warriors; obsessed with extreme cyberware and "meat-hating" philosophy. Locked in escalating war with the Inquisitors.

- Gang: Tyger Claws
  Type: Protector gang
  Turf: Japantown / Asian community
  Notes: Formerly under Arasaka, now independent. Fast bikes, boosted reflexes, killer martial arts. Helped rebuild Japantown.

- Gang: Voodoo Boys
  Type: Terror gang
  Turf: originally Florida/Caribbean crime links
  Notes: Ritual-magic overtones; deal non-synthetic drugs. Now in conflict with Haitian and Dominican refugees who resent the name.

- Gang: Iron Sights
  Type: Combat gang
  Notes: Small but tough, borderline cyberpsychos. Once Arasaka-funded; rumored to have a new benefactor.

- Gang: Inquisitors
  Type: Religious cult gang (anti-cyberware)
  Notes: Believe cyberware is blasphemy and tear it from victims to "save" their souls. At war with every other gang; universally hated.

- Gang: Red Chrome Legion
  Type: Neo-fascist hate gang
  Notes: Young extremists in uniforms and slogans; attack anything they deem not "right."

- Gang: Reckoners
  Type: Apocalyptic cult
  Notes: Preach the coming "Harvest of Souls," recruit the homeless, and rob for "donations." Some want to bring the End Times with blood and C4.

- Gang: Piranhas
  Type: Party gang
  Notes: Drink, drug, deal, and mug — "the Party is everything."

- Gang: Bozos
  Type: Killer-clown gang
  Notes: Biosculpted as clowns; prey on victims' worst fears with sadistic "pranks." Not funny.

- Gang: Prime-Time Players
  Type: Poser / family gang
  Notes: Biosculpted to resemble old TV/sitcom characters; territorial and protective, split into show-based factions.

- Gang: Philharmonic Vampyres
  Type: Prankster gang
  Notes: Theatrical vampire-themed stunt gang; ambiguous whether artists, hoodlums, or provocateurs.

- Group: Scavvers
  Type: Not a true gang
  Notes: Desperate homeless who scavenge the ruined Zones for salvage (copper, tech, weapons). Many die digging into unsafe terrain.

# LOCAL NOMAD PRESENCE
- Aldecaldo Camp: large multicultural encampment at the city's edge; the Aldecaldos' trade, transport, and Badlands-guide hub, and their main point of business in Night City.
- Steel Vaqueros: a Nomad pack out of Salinas running the coastal highway from the Santa Cruz agriplots to the southern cities; supply deals with several Night City Reclaimer groups.

# NOTE ON CORPORATE PRESENCE
Most major NeoCorps keep a Night City branch (Militech at the NorCal base, Petrochem and SovOil across the street from each other, Biotechnica's dome campus, Raven Microcyb, Network 54, Zhirafa, Trauma Team Tower, etc.). Ziggurat and Danger Gal are headquartered in the city itself.
