# Night City History

Cyberpunk Red — The City's Own Timeline

## Origin: The Morro Bay Massacre (1990)

- Night City sits on the site of Morro Bay, a small Central California coastal town on Highway 1 / U.S. 101.
- During the Collapse, a turf war between local law enforcement and Hell's Angels-legacy boostergangs spilled over. The Boosters seized the town in a five-day rampage; most of its ~10,000 residents were killed, maimed, or fled. It became a ghost town known for the Morro Bay Massacre.

## Founding: A City of Dreams

- Richard Night, a visionary developer (founder of Halsey, Ferris & Night and Night Industries), wanted to build a planned, self-sufficient city safe from the Collapse.
- He bought the ruined Morro Bay cheap. Backers: Petrochem (offshore oil/port interests) and Merrill, Asukaga & Finch (wanting a financial hub). Arasaka was hired to clear out the boostergangs.
- To escape the Massacre's stigma, the partnership renamed the area Del Coronado Bay and the city Coronado. Locals called it "Night's City" after its outsized founder.
- Construction: Night leveled surrounding hills for fill, reshaped the bay, and dredged the harbor. He designed the city as themed cultural neighborhoods (Little Italy, Japantown, Little China, Studio City, etc.) around a glittering Corporate Center of "starscrapers."

## The Mob Moves In and Night Dies (1994–1998)

- The partnership lacked construction muscle and turned to the West Coast Mobs (Mafia, Yakuza), who controlled the unions and building agencies. They invested hoping to own future vice rackets.
- Night's advanced techniques cut out Mob-controlled unions, and his insistence on a crime-free city angered them. He faced years of threats and sabotage; his corporate allies answered ruthlessly.
- Sept 20, 1998: Richard Night was assassinated in his penthouse in Parkview Tower. His killer was never caught. In his memory, the City Council renamed Coronado City "Night City."

## Mob Rule: The Dark Ages (1998–2009)

- With Night dead, the gang bosses took over the project. By 2005 the Mob ruled; corporations withdrew to their outlying Beavervilles and Corporate Plaza fortresses, uninterested in governing.
- Mob-picked puppets replaced city government. Drugs, extortion, prostitution, and cyber-terror became the norm. Notorious gangs like the Blood Razors and Slaughterhouse emerged.
- By 2009 the name "Night City" meant hell on earth — more murders in a few years than most cities saw in a decade.

## Corporate Takeover (2009–2013)

- The Corporations eradicated Mob rule in 2009, seizing control of the city.
- By 2013 they had crushed the worst violence and restored basic Police, Fire, and services. Oppressive, but safer than the Mob years.
- Their draconian housing policies (evict-and-upgrade) created the city's chronic homeless problem, as affordable housing was converted to barrack security forces.

## The Golden Age (2020)

- By 2020 Night City was a booming, gritty, stylish urban region — the quintessential city of the Cyberpunk future. Violent, but economically strong in the Corporate sector.
- Free State status: Night City lay in independent territory, not under direct U.S. control.
- Carbon Plague (2020): a corporate AV crash released a nanotech plague on the city's outskirts. It ravaged the area for two weeks, then mysteriously stopped.

## The 4th Corporate War in Night City (2022–2023)

- Because it was a Free City, Night City hosted units of both Arasaka and Militech. The War was brutal here — street-to-street, building-to-building fighting, worst around the Corporate Zone.
- Civilians fled in droves toward safer districts like Heywood and Pacifica. Much of the city was wrecked even before the Nuke.

## The Bomb (August 20, 2023)

- Event: an incursion team (Solo Morgan Blackhand and Rockerboy Johnny Silverhand among them) raided the Arasaka Towers to steal or destroy Arasaka's secure database.
- Weapon: a small pocket nuke (~0.1 kiloton), prematurely detonated at floor 120 in Kei Arasaka's bunker.
- Result: the Towers split and collapsed; central Night City became rubble in seconds. ~500,000 died instantly, ~250,000 more in the aftermath. Because the city sat on fill, the blast triggered a minor earthquake that liquefied ground and flooded the inner city.
- Blame was never settled between Militech and Arasaka. The event ended the War and opened the Time of the Red.

## Rebuilding (2023–2045)

- The wreckage itself became fill for new mega-buildings rising over the old city.
- The Pacifica Confederation — a local alliance of Pacific Northwest Free States — formed and greatly aided the rebuilding.
- Night City was abandoned by President Kress's "New" United States and has no intention of rejoining it. It dreams of becoming the biggest, most important city on the planet.

## Night City Today (2045)

- Governance: no central government and no real mayor (a ceremonial title only). The Night City Council is made up of City Managers drawn from each district — including the Combat Zones, where managers have no real control over the gangs.
- Power blocs on the Council: the Nomad Families; Edgerunners (Solos, Netrunners, Rockerboys, etc.); the old city government; and Corporate interests (Biotechnica, Militech, Petrochem, SovOil, Trauma Team, Network 54, Zhirafa, and covertly Arasaka).
- No single faction dominates. Disputes are frequent and often settled on The Street with weapons, yet things somehow get done through shifting alliances.
- Services: underfunded but heavily armed Police, Fire, and EMS, paid for by a levy on Corps and neighborhoods. Shifting ripperdoc clinics provide most medical care; a large cryobody bank remains from the corpse-choked post-Fall days.
- Layout: old neighborhoods were rearranged, renamed, or scraped away if too radioactive. The region is now described by zone type — Hot Zone (blasted nuke terrain), Rebuilding Urban Center, Executive Zone, Overpacked Suburbs, Reclaimed Perimeter, and The Open Road.
