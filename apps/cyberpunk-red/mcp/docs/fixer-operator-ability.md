# Fixer Role Ability: Operator

What the GM needs to know to adjudicate a Fixer at the table.

## What It Is

The Fixer is the middleman of the Dark Future — a black-market broker who sources goods, favors, and information through a vast web of contacts and clients. Their Role Ability is Operator, rated by an Operator Rank from 1 to 10. Operator is not a combat power; it governs what the Fixer can find, who they know, and how good a deal they can strike.

## Why It Matters at the Table

In the Time of the Red, gear is scarce. Without a Fixer, a character can only buy items up to the Premium price category; anything Expensive or higher is simply unavailable to them. The Fixer's Operator Rank is what unlocks high-end weapons, armor, cyberware, and vehicles for the whole crew. When players want something rare or pricey, the Fixer (and their Rank) is usually the answer to "can we even get this?"

(Item costs and price categories live in the core items doc / weapon-damage reference.)

## The Four Parts of Operator

- **Contacts & Clients**: The circle of people the Fixer can reach for goods, favors, or information. Higher Rank means more important contacts — from a neighborhood gang lord at low Rank up to Corp heads and world leaders at the top. The Fixer still pays for whatever they source.
- **Reach**: The highest price category of item the Fixer can ALWAYS source for clients, piece by piece, even when it's otherwise unavailable. This is the gear-gating dial (see rank table).
- **Haggle**: The Fixer's ability to strike a deal (see the roll below). Each Rank tier grants a specific bargaining perk.
- **Grease**: The Fixer's ability to blend into the many cultures of The Street — knowing the languages, social codes, and status marks of different groups. Higher Rank means fluency with more cultures (and free bonus languages), up to blending seamlessly with almost any group.

## The Haggle Roll (the one mechanic to resolve)

When a Fixer haggles a deal, it's an opposed check:

- Fixer rolls: COOL + Trading + Operator Rank + 1d10
- Opponent rolls: NPC Modifier Lookup + 1d10
- Higher total wins; on a tie the opponent (defender) wins.

On success, the Fixer completes 1 deal of their Operator Rank or lower, and applies the Haggle perk for their Rank tier. Only ONE Fixer deal can be made per transaction.

(For the opposed-roll flow and when to pause for a player roll, use the combat/opposed-roll guidance.)

## Operator Rank Progression (summary)

| Rank | Reach | Contacts | Haggle | Grease |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Everyday | Local honchos, gang lords, neighborhood leaders. | 10% better price buying or selling. | Knows the local neighborhood and its gangs. |
| 3-4 | Expensive | City gang honchos, minor politicians, Corp execs. | Buy 5+ of the same item, get one free. | Fluent in 1 other local culture (+1 free language). |
| 5-6 | No extra benefit | Major city players and politicos. | Negotiate a Job's pay-per-person up 20%. | 3 cultures total (+free languages). |
| 7-8 | Very Expensive | Local Corp presidents, mayors, city managers. | Buy Luxury/Super Luxury on credit (half now, half in a month; default once and no one repeats the deal). | 6 cultures total. |
| 9 | Luxury piece by piece | Divisional Corp heads, zone politicos, celebrities. | 20% better price buying or selling. | Blends with Corporate and governmental agencies too. |
| 10 | Super Luxury piece by piece | Major world leaders, Corp heads, world-famous celebrities. | Double a Dangerous Job's pay-per-person. | Blends seamlessly with almost any group, including secret societies and cults. |

## Running It as GM

- When players want gear, ask for the Fixer's Reach if you don't know it: at or below their Reach category, they can source it (for the price).
- Call for a Haggle roll only when there's a real negotiation and an opposing party — a willing seller at a fair price needs no roll. Narrate the contact, the meet, and the terms; use the roll to decide how the deal tilts.
- Treat Contacts and Grease as narrative permissions, not rolls: they tell you who the Fixer plausibly knows and which rooms they can walk into without friction. Let them open doors and shape scenes rather than triggering checks.
- Keep the web alive: a Fixer's deals create debts, favors, and rivals. Deals sourced now are hooks later.
