# Null & Vidik's Flat
Not long ago Null had began renting out a dirt-cheap space to serve as a quiet, secure place to hole up. After we started working together, I began crashing there, and we have since turned it into a humble base of operations for us. It's not so much a "flat" as it is a cramped basement, but we've turned it into a place to work, sleep, plan, and recuperate.

## Location
In the Heywood Industrial zone, in a not-so-populated area full of warehouses, dingy mech shops, and crumbling apartments, there is a quiet alley lit by sputtering sodium lamps. A set of stairs leads downward under one of the buildings, leading to a what looks like a series of dimly-lit maintenance hallways. After a series of confusing turns one comes to a non-descript steel door with chipped paint. Home sweet home.

## Layout
The front door opens to a wide, main low-ceilinged room to which five smaller, cramped rooms are attached. The main room is a kitchen and common area. Null and I each take one room for sleep and personal belongings, and one room for our workstations. The fifth room is a shared space for miscellaneous purposes.

## Functionality & Furniture
The kitchen is basic, meeting minimal requirements, filling the far third of the common area. The other two-thirds are an open space with a dingy couch and concrete slab "coffee table", some metal console tables along the walls and simple storage lockers. Our rooms for sleeping have simple steel-framed beds, a small table and chair, and personal items.

## Vibe & Decor
This was never meant to be a place to live - the walls, floors and ceilings are concrete, with dark, weathered, chipped paint. No windows - it's basement-level. Our workstations are the only thing we've spent money on; everything else in the apartment is humble, worn, and functional. Lighting is simple and functional but gives a cozy, gritty atmosphere that we've come to love. We've thrown down rugs in key places.

## Vidik's Workspace
- Description: A cramped sideroom with just enough room for a sturdy, functional tech-table and a well-worn chair. The table has a surface of old plasteel, weathered but impenetrable. Materials and half-finished projects lay strewn around the edges. A single light, warm, orange, industrial-bare, hangs overhead. Tools hang on a metal grid on the wall in front of the desk. A few drawers are half-open, showing their cornocopias of wires, adapters, circuit modules, and other gizmos in wonderful ordered chaos. A single monitor screen bobs on a wall sponson, attached to my humble computer I use for designs and electrical monitoring.
- Capabilities: Vidik has a stache of tech crafting materials worth about 2000eb total. He has the tools needed to make almost any tech item that can fit in the cramped space of the room.

## Null's Workspace
- Description: A slightly larger rectangular sideroom, the far side occupied by a slick computer station. A black, high-backed mesh chair in front of a tall, impressive display of four large, flat monitors on wall sponsons. The desk has a keyboard and input devices, and various bits of correspondence scattered about. A small stack of parallel CPUs stands against the wall, each emitting red light from LED bars that gives the room a red glow. Shelves of correspondence, data chips, burner phones, earpieces, and trinkets.
- Capabilities: Null has access to several corners of the interweb, trade webs, job postings, communication servers, and other cyber-nexuses of information. Has sophisticated software for comm-related technologies like encryption/decryption, source masking, and the like. This is not a hacker's computer - it is a listener's, a finder's, a mover's and... a fixer's.
