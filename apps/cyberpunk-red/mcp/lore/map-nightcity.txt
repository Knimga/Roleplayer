# NIGHT CITY MAP
# Cyberpunk Red — Geography, Zones, and Neighborhoods (2045)

# OVERVIEW
Night City sits on the Central California coast, built around Del Coronado Bay (the former Morro Bay). Much of the central city stands on landfill dumped into the bay during construction — low-lying ground (~5m elevation) that the 2023 Nuke's quake liquefied, so the core now floods with the tide. Surrounding the fill-built lowland core are hills (home to the rich and the Corporate elite), the dry scrub of the Badlands beyond, coastal cliffs to the west, and highways radiating outward.

Atmosphere: a city of violent contrasts. Glittering, crane-covered reconstruction rises beside blasted radioactive ruins; gated Executive luxury sits a maglev ride from refugee tent cities. The skies still carry the fading red tint of the Time of the Red. Danger changes block to block depending on which gang, Corp, or faction holds the turf. The NCART subway still runs when it isn't flooded. Night City is a fiercely independent Free City, abandoned by the U.S. and run by a fractious Council — rebuilding, dangerous, and dreaming of becoming the biggest city on Earth.

# HOW THE CITY IS ORGANIZED
Locals no longer use the old 2020 neighborhood names. Instead the region is divided into seven Zones, each named for the type of environment that dominates it. Each Zone also carries a Threat Rating assigned by the City Council — a measure of how dangerous it is. A single Zone may contain pockets of other Threat Ratings, especially where reconstruction or gentrification is underway.

# ZONE TYPES (7)
- Hot Zone: The area most affected by the Nuke. Blasted terrain of wrecked skyscrapers, burned-out vehicles, and entombed bodies.
- Rebuilding Urban Center: Parts that escaped the worst of the War and bomb. Cranes and construction gear everywhere, raising new Corporate towers and megabuildings.
- Executive Zone: A cordoned-off, gated area reserved for high-level Corporate executives and their families.
- Combat Zone: The most dangerous, lawless part of the city, grown from the original pre-War Combat Zone. Justice comes only from the barrel of a gun.
- Overpacked Suburbs: Former safe "Beavervilles" now packed with tent cities and unregulated refugee camps. On the edge of disaster.
- Reclaimed Perimeter: The area just outside the sprawl. On the edge of lawlessness except where the Nomad Families hold control.
- The Open Road: The open highway — only people in transit and roving boostergangs.

# THREAT RATINGS (5)
- Executive: Secure gated neighborhood, accessible only to the ultra-rich under armed security. Linked to the Rebuilding Urban Center by maglev.
- Corporate: Patrolled by Corporate security; safe from Combat Zone carnage but carries its own well-funded dangers.
- Moderate: Zones bleeding out from Corporate areas, where those who can't afford the Corporate Zone live in relative safety. Combat Zone violence sometimes leaks in.
- Combat: The worst places in the city. People die and no one cleans it up.
- Outskirts: Just outside the sprawl. The only laws are the ones you make yourself; expect road gangers and Nomads.

# ================================
# THE SEVEN ZONES AND THEIR NEIGHBORHOODS
# ================================

# THE HOT ZONE
- Zone Type: Hot Zone
- Threat Rating: Combat
- Location: Dead center of the city — the old central Corporate Zone, on the bay fill.
- Description: A haunted wasteland of twisted skyscrapers, burned-out vehicles, and buried bodies. Much was bulldozed into the Bay as fill; the rest is abandoned to the worst gangs. Radiation has subsided but lingers. You come here only if you're a ganger, suicidal, or hunting something buried in the wreckage.
Sub-neighborhoods (all ruins):
- Old Bank Block: The former banking district, mostly rubble, never officially cleared.
- Old City Center: Radioactive ruins of the once-bustling civic core.
- Old Corporate Center: The former heart of Night City's Corporations, now a ruin.
- Old Medical Center: Irradiated; its hospitals lie buried under rubble.

# THE REBUILDING URBAN CENTER
- Zone Type: Rebuilding Urban Center
- Threat Rating: Corporate / Moderate
- Location: The surviving urban core ringing the Hot Zone.
- Description: The densely built skyscraper districts that survived the blast. Cranes, Zhirafa construction mecha, private armies, and sentry drones are everywhere as new towers and megabuildings rise. Damaged seawalls mean periodic flooding; power and water are unreliable. The NCART subway runs when not tide-flooded.
Sub-neighborhoods:
- The Glen: The new civic district; holds most of Night City's government buildings (City Hall, courts, main police precinct).
- Little Europe: A divided district of tight-knit neighborhoods mixing old brick buildings and skyscrapers.
- NorCal Military Base: A heavily fortified Militech-staffed base north of the city; mostly sealed off from the rest.
- Pacifica Playground: A Corporate-sponsored district built around the Playland by the Sea amusement park; under heavy development. Coastal, to the west.
- Upper Marina: A bustling waterfront district mixing old industrial zones and gentrified "International"-style neighborhoods around a maintained marina.
- University District: A slim district on the edge of the Combat Zones; home to the city's only still-functioning university (a lively counterculture).
- Watson Development (aka Kabuki): A developing district of megabuildings and arcologies housing War refugees; much of the city's Asian population has relocated here.

# THE EXECUTIVE ZONE
- Zone Type: Executive Zone
- Threat Rating: Executive
- Location: Carved out of the open hills surrounding the city, outside the urban core; linked to the Rebuilding Urban Center by maglev/bullet train.
- Description: A single gated, heavily defended district for high-ranking Corporate execs and their families. Luxury homes, golf courses, private spas, and clubs under constant, paranoid Corporate surveillance. The area known as Beaverville (patrolled by Lazarus Corp security) sits here. Drawing a weapon brings armed security in seconds.

# THE COMBAT ZONES
- Zone Type: Combat Zone
- Threat Rating: Combat
- Location: Inner-ring abandoned blocks and slums bordering the surviving urban core.
- Description: Gangs are the absolute rulers here, dividing turf among the tenements and ruins and killing anything in the way. No reconstruction happens — occasionally Corporate or City Lawmen sweep in to cull gangers wholesale.
Sub-neighborhoods:
- Old Japantown: A former Japanese cultural center wrecked in the War; most residents fled to Watson, leaving it to the gangs.
- Little China: A sprawling, dangerous Chinatown whose small communities fight to survive against the gangs.
- Old Combat Zone: The long-abandoned district that the rest of the Combat Zones grew out of.
- South Night City: An industrial sprawl of trigger-happy gangs and abandoned warehouses turned hideouts. Port area to the south.

# THE OVERPACKED SUBURBS
- Zone Type: Overpacked Suburbs
- Threat Rating: Moderate / Combat
- Location: The ring of former suburban "Beavervilles" surrounding the core.
- Description: After the War these once-safe suburbs lost power, water, and sewer service and filled with tent cities and refugee camps. Crowded, crime-ridden, and perpetually on the edge of disaster while mega-buildings are raised to house the displaced.
Sub-neighborhoods:
- Heywood: A hugely overpacked sector holding much of the city's population; splitting by wealth — rich in the north, poor in the south (who call it Santo Domingo). East of Del Coronado Bay.
- Heywood Industrial Zone: The city's largest industrial zone — warehouses, factories, construction yards, even derelict cargo ships.
- New Westbrook: An urban sprawl built in the remains of the original Executive Zone; still glitzy but now packed with the homeless. Across the Bay on the hills.
- Rancho Coronado: A vast stretch of old Beaverville tract housing overtaken by tent cities and industrial zones. South of the city.

# THE RECLAIMED PERIMETER
- Zone Type: Reclaimed Perimeter
- Threat Rating: Outskirts
- Location: The outer towns beyond the city sprawl, up and down the coast.
- Description: Smaller towns and suburbs abandoned during 2000–2020 due to gogang raids. Reclaimers, backed by Nomad Families and private security, are rebuilding homes here for the city's dispossessed.
Neighboring cities: Atascadero, Avila Beach, Cambria, Los Osos, Paso Robles, Pismo Beach, San Luis Obispo, San Simeon.

# THE OPEN ROAD
- Zone Type: The Open Road
- Threat Rating: Outskirts
- Location: The highways radiating out from the region.
- Description: Once the domain of roving gogangs and armored Nomad caravans. As the Nomad Families take over commerce and shipping, they use surplus military hardware to drive out the gangs and make the roads safe — shifting from "Mad Max" toward "Route 66." Still dusty and littered with burned-out wrecks.
Highways included: CA-41, CA-46, CA-58, CA-99, CA-828, Interstate 5, US-101.
