# CORE ITEMS
# Cyberpunk Red — Descriptions and Costs
# Currency: Eurobucks (eb). Descriptions only; no game mechanics.

# ================================
# WEAPON ATTACHMENTS
# ================================
- Bayonet: A blade fixed to a long gun so it can also be used as a hand weapon. Can't be concealed while attached. — 100eb
- Drum Magazine: An oversized drum-shaped magazine holding far more rounds than standard. Can't be concealed. — 500eb
- Extended Magazine: A lengthened magazine holding extra rounds. Can't be concealed. — 100eb
- Grenade Launcher Underbarrel: A single-shot grenade launcher mounted beneath the barrel of a long gun. Can't be concealed. — 500eb
- Shotgun Underbarrel: A short two-shot shotgun mounted beneath the barrel of a long gun. Can't be concealed. — 500eb
- Infrared Nightvision Scope: A scope that lets the shooter see through darkness, smoke, and fog, distinguishing warm bodies from cold metal. — 500eb
- Sniping Scope: A high-magnification scope for spotting and hitting targets at long range. — 100eb
- Smartgun Link: A targeting link that turns a weapon into a Smartgun, feeding aiming data directly to a user wired to receive it. Takes about an hour to install or remove. — 500eb

# ================================
# AMMUNITION
# Bullets, shells, and arrows are sold in lots of 10; grenades and rockets are sold individually.
# ================================
- Basic Ammunition: Standard rounds with no special properties. — 10eb
- Rubber Ammunition: Less-lethal rounds meant to subdue rather than kill. — 10eb
- Armor-Piercing Ammunition: Rounds built to punch through armor more effectively. — 100eb
- Expansive Ammunition: Rounds that expand on impact to worsen wounds. — 100eb
- Incendiary Ammunition: Rounds that set the target ablaze. — 100eb
- Poison Ammunition: Rounds that deliver poison into the target. — 100eb
- Flashbang Ammunition: Grenades that blind and deafen rather than wound. — 100eb
- Smoke Ammunition: Rounds/grenades that lay down a concealing smoke screen. — 50eb
- Teargas Ammunition: Rounds/grenades that disperse an irritant gas. — 50eb
- Biotoxin Ammunition: Arrows and grenades that deliver a lethal toxin instead of impact damage. — 500eb
- EMP Ammunition: Grenades that disrupt and disable cyberware and electronics. — 500eb
- Sleep Ammunition: Rounds carrying a sedative to knock targets unconscious. — 500eb
- Smart Ammunition: Self-correcting rounds that work in concert with a Smartgun. — 500eb

# ARMOR (MASTER LIST)
# ================================
- Leathers: Thin leather with reinforced pads at shoulders, hips, and gut. Favored by Nomads and bikers. Light, unobtrusive protection. — 20eb
- Kevlar: The old standby fiber; can be tailored into clothes, jackets, business suits, even bikinis. Light protection. — 50eb
- Light Armorjack: Kevlar and plastic mesh woven into fabric. Solid everyday street protection with no movement penalty. — 100eb
- Bodyweight Suit: A comfortable, breathable skinsuit lined with impact-absorbing armorgel; also holds a Netrunner's deck and cabling. Worn as a single head-and-body piece. — 1,000eb
- Medium Armorjack: Heavier Armorjack with solid plastic plating over thicker Kevlar mesh. Good protection, some bulk. — 100eb
- Heavy Armorjack: The thickest Armorjack, denser Kevlar layered with plastic and mesh. Stops all but the heaviest attacks; noticeably restrictive. — 500eb
- Flak: A modern flak vest and pants with metal plates; built to shrug off explosives, shotguns, and heavier rounds. Bulky and restrictive. — 500eb
- Metalgear: Solid metal and plastic plates over a mesh bodysuit. Stops almost anything, but makes the wearer slow and easy to hit. — 5,000eb
- Bulletproof Shield: A transparent polycarbonate shield that soaks incoming fire until it breaks. Occupies one arm. — 100eb
