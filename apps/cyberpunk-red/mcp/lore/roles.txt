# ROLES
Roles are equivalent to classes in other roleplaying games. Each Character has a Role that defines their profession, identity, and specialty on The Street. Every Role grants a unique Role Ability.

# ROCKERBOY
Summary: Street poet, musician, and social agitator who challenges Corporate and government authority through performance, message, and presence.
Role Ability: Charismatic Impact
- Influences Fans (individuals or groups) through music, art, poetry, dance, or personality
- Can convert non-fans into Fans, then call on Fans for favors
- Checks rolled vs DV8 (single fan), DV10 (small group, up to 6), DV12 (huge group)

# SOLO
Summary: Elite combat specialist - hired gun, bodyguard, corporate enforcer, or freelance killer, often ex-military and heavily augmented.
Role Ability: Combat Awareness
- Points distributed among combat abilities: Damage Deflection, Fumble Recovery, Initiative Reaction, Precision Attack, Spot Weakness, Threat Detection
- Reassigned at the start of combat, outside combat, or using an Action in combat

# NETRUNNER
Summary: Hacker and master of the post-NET cyberverse, jacking in on-site to breach systems, steal data, and battle Black ICE.
Role Ability: Interface
- Enables Netrunning and determines NET Actions per Turn (2 at Rank 1-3, up to 5 at Rank 10)
- Grants access to Interface Abilities: Backdoor, Cloak, Control, Eye-Dee, Pathfinder, Scanner, Slide, Virus, Zap

# TECH
Summary: Renegade mechanic and inventor who builds, fixes, modifies, and creates technology to keep the Dark Future running.
Role Ability: Maker
- Each Rank grants 1 Rank in two Maker Specialties: Field Expertise, Upgrade Expertise, Fabrication Expertise, Invention Expertise
- Field Expertise allows temporary full repairs and bonuses to Tech Skill Checks

# MEDTECH
Summary: Street doctor and cyberware medic who patches up meat and metal alike, sometimes affiliated with Trauma Team.
Role Ability: Medicine
- Each Rank adds a point to one of three Specialties: surgery, pharmaceuticals, cryosystems operation
- Allows keeping critically wounded people alive beyond normal means

# MEDIA
Summary: Reporter, journalist, or influencer who digs up and publishes the truth (or a version of it) about Corporate and government corruption.
Role Ability: Credibility
- Determines Access/Sources, Audience, Believability, and Impact of published stories
- Grants passive rumor detection (GM rolls Credibility + 1d10 vs Rumor Table DVs) and active rumor-hunting via Skills like Library Search, Conversation, Interrogation

# EXEC
Summary: Corporate climber navigating Megacorp politics, projects, and internal power struggles, often via extraction and sabotage operations.
Role Ability: Teamwork
- Builds and manages a team of subordinates with visible job titles and covert secondary roles (e.g. secretary who is secretly a Netrunner)
- Team members come with free housing and clothing

# LAWMAN
Summary: Police officer or law enforcement agent patrolling the ruins of Night City, hunting criminals, gangs, and cyberpsychos.
Role Ability: Backup
- Calls in fellow officers for support, armed and armored based on Lawman's Rank
- Higher Ranks summon heavier units (up to national law enforcement/Interpol/FBI/Netwatch strike teams)

# FIXER
Summary: Dealmaker, black-market broker, and information hub connecting clients, goods, and favors across Night City's criminal and legitimate economies.
Role Ability: Operator
- Contacts & Clients: who the Fixer can reach for goods, favors, or info
- Reach: highest Price Category the Fixer can always source
- Haggle: opposed COOL + Trading + Operator Rank check to strike favorable deals
- Grease: ability to blend into different cultures and social circles

# NOMAD
Summary: Road warrior and transport specialist belonging to a Nomad Family/Pack, moving people, cargo, and supplies across the wastes.
Role Ability: Moto
- Adds Moto Rank to Drive Land Vehicle, Pilot Air Vehicle, Pilot Sea Vehicle, and related Vehicle Tech Skill Checks
- Each Rank increase adds a vehicle to the Family Motorpool or upgrades an existing Family Vehicle
